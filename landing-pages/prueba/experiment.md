## Hypothesis

Recently graduated ophthalmologists opening their own practice will respond better to “organize the practice from the first patient and stay on the same platform as you grow” than to messaging that Alida is free.

## Audience

Ophthalmologists in Mexico who just finished residency or are starting a private practice (one doctor, own or rented consultorio).

## Problem

Opening a practice forces them to improvise scheduling, records, and prescriptions (paper, Excel, WhatsApp, or a generic EMR). Later they have to migrate when an assistant, a second site, or surgery shows up.

## Why We Believe This

- The Alida customer baseline is almost entirely ophthalmology. The easiest profile to reach with ads is the solo consultorio (one doctor, 0–1 assistant).
- What active clinics actually use is agenda + expediente (sometimes surgery). Finance is almost unused — do not lead with it.
- “Free” is already the homepage and the pilot plan. This experiment tests whether the *reason to start* (day one + no migration) converts better than price.
- We have not shown that our best current customers are recent graduates. This page tests a reachable segment; it does not declare the ICP.

## Value Proposition

Alida is built for the consultorio they open today (agenda + expediente), and the same system scales to assistants, more doctors, and hospital-level practice. Do not lead with “free.” Do not say it is “not for hospitals.”

## Primary CTA

One action: create an account on the free plan.

- URL: `https://app.alidahealth.com/users/sign_up?referral_code=ALIDAFREEPLAN`
- Button copy (Spanish, on the page): “Empieza con tu primer paciente” — not “Crea una cuenta gratuita”
- No Typeform, no “talk to sales”

## Traffic Source

Not set yet. Planned: one ads channel (Meta) pointing at this URL. Do not link it from the homepage nav.

## Success Metrics

Define the numeric bar in Phase 4 of the acquisition cycle before spending. Until then, measure:

- Landing → CTA click (`cta_clicked` / Meta `CTAClicked`: `placement`, `label`, `landing`, `referral_code`)
- CTA → signup
- Signup → first appointment (activation)
- Is the lead a recently graduated ophthalmologist / new consultorio?
- After signup / interview: did they want a way to start, or to look established with patients? (CEO split — do not change this page to test it)
